## Top findings

### Roof model (production in legend-ocr-count) — threshold is the dominant problem
- **20.8% of all GT symbols are found by the model but dropped by the 0.35 cutoff.**
  Worst: PLUMBING STACK (recall 0.23 at 0.35 vs 0.79 at 0.05, 26 threshold misses at
  conf 0.06–0.30) and PIPE PENETRATION (0.21 vs 0.62, 14 threshold misses).
- PIPE PENETRATION is also borderline tiny (median 16px at inference scale — at the
  P3 stride-8 floor) and gets confused with ROOF DRAIN (4x). P2-head retrain candidate.
- HATCH DETAIL (4 train instances), HEAT DETAIL (2), SKYLIGHT (5), PIPE HOUSING (1)
  are data-scarce; their misses are expected, not bugs.
- TTA adds ~2.5% recall (PLUMBING STACK +4, PIPE PENETRATION +2).

### Tech model — healthy overall (92.7%), misses concentrate in two patterns
- **DATA PERMANENT LINK: 26 of 28 misses are truly undetected, 18 of them within 5%
  of an image edge** — the clearest tile-edge/overlap failure (symbol ~47px vs 65px
  production overlap). Increase tile overlap or check the exclusive-region merge in
  `legend-ocr-count/pipeline/tile_overlap.py`.
- SURFACE REACEWAY WM 5400 / WM2300: threshold + edge misses; TTA recovers most
  (WM5400 reaches 0.994 recall with TTA). Elongated raceways at 63px vs 65px overlap.
- J HOOK: median box 238px — far larger than the 65px overlap; any tile boundary
  cuts it. Recall 0.2 despite 365 train instances.
- `classroom 15` and `object` have 1 train instance each — remove or relabel these classes.

## Recommended fixes (in order of payoff)

1. **Roof model: rebalance conf.** Lowering prod conf from 0.35 toward ~0.20 lifts overall
   recall 67% -> ~85% on this eval; better long-term fix is more labeled examples of
   PLUMBING STACK / PIPE PENETRATION to push their confidence up (train counts 96 / 58).
2. **Increase tile overlap** above the largest common symbol (currently 65px; raceways are
   ~63px, J HOOK 238px, DATA PERMANENT LINK 47px but edge-clustered). Overlap of
   1.5x the largest expected symbol implies raising `roi_zoom.overlap_pct` in
   `drawing-zoom-split/config/defaults.yaml` (0.10 -> ~0.25 minimum).
3. **Label more data** for the few-shot roof classes (HATCH DETAIL, HEAT DETAIL, SKYLIGHT,
   PIPE HOUSING) — misses there are expected with 1–5 train instances.
4. **Enable TTA (`augment=True`)** where latency allows — +2.5% recall roof, +0.9% tech,
   no retraining.
5. **P2-head retrain (yolov8-p2.yaml) only for the roof model** — PIPE PENETRATION is the
   single class at the stride-8 size floor. Not needed for the tech model (median sizes
   are all >45px at inference scale).
